## Steps to reproduce

1. Create sellers `S00001` and `S00002` in `T1000`.
2. Post a SALE of 10,000.00 split 70/30 dated 2026-10-05.
3. Post a RETURN of 1,000.00 for that sale dated 2026-11-04 (30 days later).

## Expected result

Credit lines -700.00 for S00001 and -300.00 for S00002 (the window is 30 days or less).

## Actual result

201 with no credit lines, as if the return were outside the window.

## Impact

The largest money defect of rc1. In the dataset: 30 returns on day 30, 31 statements with a wrong credited amount and 26 payouts overpaid, 6,066.83 MXN in total.

Pending developer analysis.
