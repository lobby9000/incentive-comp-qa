## Steps to reproduce

1. Create seller `S00001` and a 2026-10 quota of 10,000.00.
2. Post SALES totalling 5,000.50.
3. Get the statement.

## Expected result

`payout` "50.01" (50.005 rounds half-up).

## Actual result

`payout` "50.00".

## Impact

One cent under on every payout whose exact value ends in 5 at the third decimal. In the dataset: 5 statements underpaid by 0.01, four of them ordinary sellers from the random data, not the crafted boundary seller.

Pending developer analysis.
