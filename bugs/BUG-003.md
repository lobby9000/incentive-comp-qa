## Steps to reproduce

1. Create seller `S00001` and a 2026-10 quota of 10,000.00.
2. Post SALES totalling 5,000.50.
3. Get the statement.

## Expected result

`payout` "50.01" (50.005 rounds half-up).

## Actual result

`payout` "50.00".

## Impact

One cent under on every payout whose exact value ends in 5 at the third decimal. In the dataset: 4 payouts underpaid by 0.01. Three of them belong to ordinary sellers from the random data, not to the crafted boundary seller.

## Root cause

`Decimal.quantize(Decimal('0.01'))` without a rounding argument uses the context default, `ROUND_HALF_EVEN` (banker's rounding).

Fix: `0ee2c3b`.

## Verification

TC-CAL-010 passes. The four affected payouts reconcile to the cent.
